Call Center Solutions, LLC (the "Company") operates a multi-tenant hosted contact center platform, marketed under the product name "ContactConnection." The Company's business customers ("tenants") use the platform to run their own customer-service and sales phone lines: primarily inbound calls, plus outbound calls placed individually by the tenant's agents (returning calls to customers, customer-requested callbacks, and order follow-ups). The Company originates outbound traffic onto the PSTN exclusively through its underlying voice service provider, SignalWire, Inc. ("SignalWire"). Each tenant is provisioned as a separate SignalWire subproject with its own telephone numbers. The Company does not sell wholesale origination to other providers and does not act as an intermediate or gateway provider for third-party traffic.

2. Commitment and Scope

The Company is committed to preventing the origination of illegal calls through its platform and to full compliance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (47 U.S.C. § 227), the Truth in Caller ID Act (47 U.S.C. § 227(e)), the Commission's implementing rules at 47 CFR § 64.1200, and the robocall mitigation requirements at 47 CFR §§ 64.6300–64.6308.

This robocall mitigation program applies to all voice traffic the Company originates. The program is reviewed at least annually and is updated when the Company's services, call patterns, regulatory obligations, or carrier relationships change, and before any new outbound calling capability (such as automated dialing) is made available to tenants. Stephen Soper, Owner/Member, owns the program and is responsible for its maintenance and enforcement.

3. Call Authentication (STIR/SHAKEN) and Basis for Non-Implementation

The Company originates all PSTN traffic as a downstream customer of SignalWire. The Company does not own, operate, or control the network infrastructure necessary to implement STIR/SHAKEN: it holds no direct access to numbering resources (all telephone numbers are obtained through SignalWire), operates no PSTN interconnection facilities, and does not control the network on which caller ID authentication is performed. The call-processing software the Company operates functions as an application and media server connected to SignalWire's SIP service, not as a facilities-based carrier network.

Calls the Company originates are authenticated under the STIR/SHAKEN framework by SignalWire, which signs calls placed through its network and determines the attestation level. SignalWire does not offer its customers the option of signing with their own certificate.

Pursuant to 47 CFR § 64.6305(d)(2)(i), the Company is unable to implement STIR/SHAKEN due to this lack of control over the network infrastructure necessary to do so. The Company therefore does not certify to full or partial STIR/SHAKEN implementation, and instead certifies to the robocall mitigation program described in this document.

Attestation and caller identification

  • Attestation is determined and applied by SignalWire. Calling numbers purchased on SignalWire are eligible for full (A) attestation following SignalWire's vetting of the Company; numbers a tenant has verified with SignalWire but that are hosted elsewhere are eligible for at most partial (B) attestation.
  • A tenant's calls can present only a telephone number purchased or verified in that tenant's own SignalWire subproject; SignalWire rejects calls presenting any other number. The Company does not offer caller ID pass-through of a third party's number.
  • The Company does not knowingly originate calls with inaccurate, false, or misleading caller identification information, and does not permit tenants to do so.

4. Reasonable Steps to Avoid Originating Illegal Traffic

4.1 Customer due diligence ("Know Your Customer")

The Company onboards every tenant directly; there is no self-service sign-up. Before a tenant's lines go live, the Company:

  • Verifies the tenant's legal business identity, physical address, and website or public business presence;
  • Identifies an authorized signer and a compliance contact for the tenant;
  • Obtains a written description of the tenant's intended use, including inbound and outbound calling, expected volumes, and — for any outbound calling — the basis on which the tenant has the consent the law requires;
  • Screens the tenant, its principals, and its use case against prohibited-use categories and publicly available enforcement and complaint information;
  • Confirms the tenant's right to use any telephone number it will present as caller ID.

Tenants presenting elevated risk (for example high-volume outbound calling, lead generation, or collections) receive enhanced review and closer ongoing monitoring, and may be required to provide consent records on request.

4.2 Contractual obligations

The Company's Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy (published at https://www.contactconnection.io/terms and /acceptable-use) require every tenant to:

  • Comply with the TCPA, the Truth in Caller ID Act, the Telemarketing Sales Rule, state telemarketing and do-not-call laws, and all applicable FCC rules;
  • Place calls only to parties from whom the tenant holds the legally required consent, and honor revocation of consent and do-not-call requests;
  • Present only telephone numbers the tenant is authorized to use;
  • Refrain from originating unlawful robocalls or spoofed calls, and from using the platform for fraud, impersonation, or harassment;
  • Maintain and produce, on the Company's request, records evidencing consent and compliance;
  • Cooperate with traceback and regulatory inquiries routed through the Company.

The Company reserves the right to throttle, suspend, or terminate a tenant — immediately and without prior notice where warranted — for violation of these terms or for originating suspected illegal traffic.

4.3 Know Your Upstream Provider

SignalWire is the Company's sole underlying voice service provider for PSTN origination and termination. SignalWire signs the Company's calls under STIR/SHAKEN, enforces that calls present only numbers held or verified in the originating subproject, and is undergoing its own vetting of the Company's business and use case for attestation. The Company does not accept or carry voice traffic originated by other providers.

4.4 Number and caller ID controls

  • Telephone numbers are obtained through SignalWire and assigned to a specific tenant's subproject;
  • Outbound calls present only numbers held or verified in the originating tenant's subproject; any other calling number is rejected by SignalWire;
  • Caller ID pass-through of a third party's number is not offered.

4.5 Traffic monitoring

The Company retains call detail records for all originated traffic and reviews them regularly for patterns indicative of illegal or fraudulent calling, including:

  • Volume that departs from a tenant's onboarded profile, or sudden spikes in outbound calls;
  • High proportions of very short calls, low answer rates, or high invalid-number rates;
  • Sequential dialing of number ranges, or rapid rotation of calling numbers;
  • Calls outside permissible calling hours for the called party's time zone;
  • Complaints or traceback requests associated with a tenant or calling number.

The Company may suspend a calling number, a campaign, or a tenant's outbound calling pending investigation. Automated alerting on these patterns will be added as outbound volume on the platform grows.

4.6 Outbound dialing, consent, calling hours, and do-not-call

The platform does not currently offer automated outbound dialing; outbound calls are placed individually by tenants' agents. Automated (progressive or predictive) dialing will be enabled for a tenant only after the Company reviews the tenant's use case and consent practices, and only with the following controls in operation: scrubbing of dialing lists against the National Do Not Call Registry and the tenant's internal do-not-call list; enforcement of permissible calling hours in the called party's time zone; and limiting predictive-dialer call abandonment to the legal maximum. Prerecorded-message calls are not permitted without documented prior express written consent. Tenants remain contractually responsible for consent and for compliance with federal and state do-not-call requirements.

4.7 Complaint handling

The Company maintains a monitored address, abuse@contactconnection.io, for complaints regarding calls originated through its platform, published on its website. Complaints are logged, associated with the responsible tenant and calling number, investigated, and used as an input to the monitoring and enforcement process described above.

5. Mitigation of Identified Illegal Traffic

When the Company identifies illegal traffic originated through its platform — whether through its own monitoring, a complaint, a traceback, or a notification from the Commission or law enforcement — it will:

  1. Promptly investigate, including review of call detail records, the tenant's onboarding file and use case, and any consent records the tenant can produce;
  2. Take effective mitigating action, which may include blocking the offending calling number or campaign, throttling or suspending the tenant's outbound calling, and, where the conduct is confirmed or the tenant is unresponsive, terminating the tenant's service;
  3. Document the investigation, the action taken, and the date, and retain that record;
  4. Where a notification from the Commission is involved, respond as described in Section 7.

6. Cooperation with Traceback

The Company commits to respond fully and within 24 hours to all traceback requests from the Commission, from civil and criminal law enforcement, and from the industry traceback consortium. Specifically, the Company will:

  • Acknowledge and substantively respond to a traceback request within 24 hours;
  • Identify the tenant that originated the traffic;
  • Take the mitigation steps in Section 5 where a traceback identifies illegal traffic the Company originated;
  • Maintain a designated traceback point of contact (Section 1) and keep that contact information current in the Robocall Mitigation Database.

Membership in the Industry Traceback Group is not a prerequisite to this cooperation, and the Company's commitment applies whether or not it is a member.

7. Response to Commission Notification of Suspected Illegal Traffic

Upon receiving a notification from the Commission under 47 CFR § 64.1200(n)(2) that the Company is originating or transmitting suspected illegal traffic, the Company will:

  1. Promptly investigate the identified traffic;
  2. Report the results of its investigation to the Commission and to the Enforcement Bureau within the timeframe specified in the notification (or, if none is specified, within 14 days);
  3. If the Company determines the identified traffic is illegal, promptly take effective mitigating action, including the steps described in Section 5, and describe that action in its report;
  4. If the Company concludes the traffic is legal, explain to the Commission why, and the steps taken to reach that conclusion, within the same timeframe.

The Company understands that failure to respond, or to mitigate confirmed illegal traffic, may result in downstream providers being authorized or required to block traffic from the Company.

8. Recordkeeping

The Company retains, for no less than 24 months: tenant onboarding and due-diligence files; call detail records for originated traffic; monitoring reviews and investigations; complaint records; traceback correspondence and responses; and Commission notifications and the Company's responses. Records are made available to the Commission and to the industry traceback consortium on request.

9. Governance and Review

Responsibility for this program rests with Stephen Soper, Owner/Member. The program and the supporting platform controls are reviewed at least annually, after any material change to the Company's services or calling patterns, and before any new outbound calling capability is made available. Personnel involved in tenant onboarding, monitoring, and abuse response are trained on these obligations.
